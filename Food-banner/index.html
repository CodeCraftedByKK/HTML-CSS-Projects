A family recipe book, rewritten

Spice & Co. began in 2012 with one tandoor and a notebook of our grandmother's recipes. We still toast and grind our spices in-house every morning.

Today our chefs mix those traditions with techniques from around the world, so every plate feels familiar and a little surprising.
